# Domain advisory

1. Identify the task, Product, Campaign if relevant, and the exact versions of approved context supplied. Work only from material scoped to this request.
2. Extract the product facts and Domain Pack rules that bear on the decision. Tag each material statement as approved fact, domain rule, observed data, external evidence, assumption, inference or open question.
3. Check terminology, buying behavior, constraints, prohibited and evidence-dependent claims, and risks. Keep product facts separate from reusable domain guidance.
4. When current public research conflicts with approved material, cite both and ask for review. An external page does not update the approved pack.
5. Choose one advisory status. `APPROVED` means this review found no material issue. `APPROVED_WITH_CONSTRAINTS` lists conditions for downstream work. `NEEDS_REVIEW` names an unresolved decision. `BLOCKED` cites the approved constraint that the proposed action would violate.
6. End with a downstream brief the lead can paste into the next specialist's message: required facts and constraints, prohibited claims, evidence to carry, and open questions. The advisory does not approve execution.
