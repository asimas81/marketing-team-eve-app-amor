# Claim review

For each claim, record its exact wording, source, scope, date when relevant, and one disposition: supported, supported with conditions, evidence required, prohibited by an approved rule, or unresolved conflict. A plausible claim remains unapproved until the Product Context or an authorized reviewer accepts its evidence. Carry qualifiers into any recommended wording. Identify the specific evidence that would settle an uncertain claim, and return wording changes as proposals rather than updates to a source document.
